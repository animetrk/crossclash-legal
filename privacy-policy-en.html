Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026
Data controller: Mert Özgürel
Contact: crossclash@proton.me

1. Scope

This policy explains how CrossClash processes personal data. Using the application does not by itself mean that you consent to personalised advertising. Where consent is required, you will be given a separate, freely given, and withdrawable choice.

2. Data stored on your device

The application stores game progress, scores, selected puzzles, sound, vibration, dark-mode settings, and language preferences locally so the game can operate and resume. You can delete local game data by uninstalling the application or clearing its data in device settings.

If you choose to send a support email, the name, email address, and message you provide are used to provide support and retained for no longer than 30 days after the request is closed.

3. When optional services are enabled

If Google Play Games or Apple Game Center is enabled, optional sign-in may process a player identifier, display name, profile image, progress, scores, achievements, and leaderboard data.

If Google Mobile Ads/AdMob is enabled, Google and selected advertising-technology partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inference, device information, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention data for serving ads, measurement, security, and—where consent is given—personalised advertising. Where required, consent and in-app privacy choices will be provided.

If Firebase analytics, crash reporting, or messaging is enabled, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before activation. If “Remove Ads” is offered, a product identifier, purchase status, and store receipt or purchase token may be processed to grant and restore the entitlement and prevent fraud. The application does not collect payment-card information.

4. Legal bases

We process data to perform a contract and provide the application or purchased product, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention, or, where required, on the basis of consent.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Depending on enabled services, data may be shared with Google LLC and affiliates, Apple Inc. and affiliates, selected AdMob partners, Firebase service providers, and legally authorised authorities. Data may be processed in Türkiye, the European Economic Area, or other countries using an applicable lawful transfer mechanism.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Send requests to crossclash@proton.me. We may request reasonable information to verify identity and will respond within 30 days at the latest.

7. Children's privacy

The application is designed for a general audience aged 9 and over. Applicable age thresholds, parental-consent requirements, and child-directed advertising rules will be followed.

8. GitHub Pages

This page is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may log a visitor's IP address for security purposes. Those technical logs are subject to GitHub's privacy policy; the Publisher does not create visitor accounts or collect form submissions on this page.

9. Security, retention, and changes

We use reasonable safeguards such as data minimisation, access restrictions, and secure transmission, but no system is completely secure. Data is retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. Material changes will be communicated through a suitable channel.
